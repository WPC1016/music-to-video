### 03 · 树与绳

**中文提示词**：
暗调场景。画面右侧是一棵光秃秃的树干，没有叶子，枝丫像枯笔画的硬线。从最低的一根树枝上垂下一根粗麻绳，绳子末端打成一个空的圈，圈里什么都没有。绳子和树的影子被正上方的冷白光投在米黄色的纸质地面上，影子边界极其锐利。**画面里没有任何人，没有任何生物。** 绳圈在轻微摆动。

**English prompt**：
dark scene, a bare leafless tree trunk on the right side, branches drawn as hard dry-brush lines, a thick hemp rope hanging from the lowest branch, ending in an empty loop with nothing inside, the rope and tree cast razor-sharp shadows on pale yellow paper ground under cold overhead light, no people no figures no animals in frame, the loop sways slightly, palette limited to newspaper yellow #E8E0CC and ink black #14110F --ar 9:16

person, human figure, body, silhouette of a person, hanging body, noose on neck, blood, blue tones, gradient, glow, text, watermark
